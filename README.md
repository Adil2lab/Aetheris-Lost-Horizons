# 🌌 Aetheris: Lost Horizons

> A lightweight, flight-focused adventure modpack centered on aerial exploration, tactical magic, 6-DOF dogfighting, and multi-dimensional dungeon raiding. Built specifically to deliver 100+ FPS on low-to-mid spec systems (4.5–5 GB RAM).

---

## 📖 Overview

**Aetheris: Lost Horizons** reimagines Minecraft exploration by trading heavy physics engines and laggy tile entities for high-speed aerial flight, fluid melee combat, and deep spellcrafting. 

Traverse vast mountain ranges with biplanes, dive through 6-DOF air maneuvers, raid sprawling floating fortresses, and conquer multi-phase dimensional titans—all without tanking your frame rate or server TPS.

---

## ✨ Core Pillars

* **🚁 6-DOF Aerial Flight:** Pilot biplanes, airships, and gyrocopters with *Immersive Aircraft* and *Do a Barrel Roll*. Enjoy smooth camera banking, pitch, yaw, and aerobatic loops with complete flight control.
* **⚔️ Tactical Combat & Martial Skills:** Replaces vanilla click-spamming with *Better Combat* animations, posture breaking, combo chains, dual-wielding, and specialized weapons from *Simply Swords*.
* **🔮 Elemental Magic:** Harness Fire, Ice, Lightning, Evocation, and Holy magic with *Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks*. Inscribe custom spell scrolls, forge spellbooks, and weave spell combos in combat.
* **🏰 Overhauled World & Sky Dungeons:** Explore realistic terrain generated by *Terralith* and *Tectonic*, discover floating *Sky Villages*, and conquer mega-structures from *When Dungeons Arise*, *YUNG's*, and *L'Ender's Cataclysm*.
* **⚡ Ultra-Low RAM Overhead:** Ground-up optimization engine using *Embeddium*, *ModernFix*, *FerriteCore*, *ImmediatelyFast*, *EntityCulling*, and *BadOptimizations*.

## ⚙️ Optimization & Setup Guide

1. **Pre-Generation (Chunky):** When starting a new world, open the console or chat and run `/chunky radius 2500` followed by `/chunky start`. Let it pre-render your surrounding world chunks to ensure smooth flight without chunk-loading stutters.
2. **OptiFine Warning:** **Do NOT add OptiFine.** OptiFine breaks *Embeddium*, *Oculus*, and *GeckoLib* rendering pipelines. Use the pre-installed *Oculus* mod if you wish to run shaders.
